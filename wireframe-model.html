Read this before trusting a number

  • In-plane (X–Z) dimensions are the solid ones. Depth (Y) layering — which plate sits where, and which side the motors are on — is assumed. Frame 2 suggests the stack motor | upper link | 18.5 mm hub | forearm at the elbow, but it can't say which way round it faces. A top-down or ~45° frame would settle it.
  • One scale for everything: 3.19 px/mm (five clicks on the 25 mm hole pitch, ±1.5%). Anything not on the plate's depth plane can be off by a few percent.
  • Outliers excluded: frame 1 bracket height (clicked short of the top) and frame 3 forearm "80.3" (line starts at the elbow pivot, not the slot end).
  • Plate: 200 × 200 is nominal (8 × 8 holes at 25 mm). The front edge measures 206.8 mm raw only because it is closer to the camera. Hole diameter, plate thickness, and the plate's depth were not measurable.
  • Mounting: the base axis lands at the centre of a 25 mm hole square, which hints at a bolt pattern through nearby plate holes. Check the video for how the base is actually fastened.
